The Town of Salisbury, Massachusetts

Property Assessment Data


DISCLAIMER:The information available on this website relates to Fiscal Year 2026 which has an assessment date of January 1, 2025. The information here reflects the ownership and how each property existed on that date.

Ownership and Sales records are updated weekly in the Assessor's Office.

Some property information may have been changed after January 1, 2025. Changes resulting from database corrections, recent building permits or the abatement process and any new construction, land subdivision or change of use occurring after January 1, 2025 do not appear on this website.

** WebPro totals are calculated on an individual card basis**

The information that you view from this website is a summary of the property data. The following information is provided as a service to the residents and property owners of Salisbury as well as the general public.

The tax bills containing these assessments will be mailed no later than December 31, 2025 and the payment will be due February 1, 2025. Salisbury has a single tax rate. The Fiscal 2026 Tax Rate is $9.93 per thousand dollars of value.
